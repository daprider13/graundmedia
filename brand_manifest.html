Mantra: "Be strong to move the world"

The famous phrase attributed to Archimedes—"Give me a lever long enough and a fulcrum on which to place it, and I shall move the world"—has been the cornerstone of Western thought on efficiency and the power of strategy.

At Graund Dsgn, we take this principle beyond mechanical physics into the realm of personal and creative growth. To move the outside world, you must first build an unbreakable strength within. Resilience, consistency, and a clear purpose act as the essential components of that lever.

Every garment and every design we create seeks to remind you that you possess the necessary tools to generate a monumental impact on your environment. We create graphics for those who forge their own path. Each collection represents a different way of facing the world, but they all stem from the same idea: strength is not just physical; it is the ability to move, create, question, and leave a mark.

Above all, we want to have fun doing it, striving to always deliver a fresh product that we want to wear ourselves, hoping that those who acquire our garments feel the exact same way.

Brand Philosophy

Graund Design Co. is not a clothing store (even if our social media says otherwise, lol); it is a creative design studio that bridges apparel and urban culture. The brand stems from the premise that true strength does not stem from external imposition, but rather from internal construction ("inner strength").

GRAUND Manifest: Cultivating Inner Strength

To change the world, power must be built from within. True strength is not just about brute force; it is a combination of resilience, clarity, and unwavering purpose.

  • Resilience: The capacity to adapt and be flexible in the face of a changing environment.
  • Focus: Directing your energy toward a single point of significant impact rather than scattering your efforts. Consistency goes a long way, transforming small steps into great paths.
  • Consistency: Applying steady, daily pressure that, over time, translates into a transcendental shift.

How to Apply Your Strength?

When you are ready to make an impact, remember these fundamental principles:

  • Find your fulcrum: Identify your core values, your unique strengths, or the exact turning point in your situation where minimum effort produces maximum results.
  • Develop your lever: Equip yourself with the knowledge, skills, and people who form your proper support network to enhance your natural capabilities.
  • Take action: Stand firm and trust the momentum you generate.
